About Me

What I love most about data science? It can advance ANY topic. My background spans mathematics, physical chemistry, and computer science, which gives me a unique perspective when tackling complex challenges. I've worked on everything from colorectal cancer prediction and energy grid optimization to nutrition tracking, housing transparency, and media bias detection. It's this interdisciplinary foundation that keeps me energized.

I thrive in ambitious environments where I can work on society-driven problems. Real impact comes from combining rigorous methodology with practical application. The Province of Utrecht adopted my energy forecasting work for infrastructure planning, which showed me how well-designed ML systems can actually support policy decisions. I particularly enjoy working on optimization problems, time-series forecasting, and building AI systems that respect user privacy.

I work well independently but really thrive in small, focused teams. Clear communication matters to me, whether I'm talking to technical colleagues or non-technical stakeholders. I'm comfortable translating complex technical work into actionable insights, and I bring both analytical rigor and product intuition to every project. I also enjoy mentoring and learning from others.

Woonbusters.nl

Solo Project

National rental-market data platform monitoring the full advertised rental supply of the Netherlands daily. 1,000+ agency sites scraped, deduplicated and tested against the legal rent-points system (WWS). Bayesian model estimating the legal maximum rent per dwelling, validated against the Huurcommissie's official check. Public quarterly market report (55,000+ listings), B2B data API for municipalities, paid subscriptions via Stripe. Built and operated entirely solo.
